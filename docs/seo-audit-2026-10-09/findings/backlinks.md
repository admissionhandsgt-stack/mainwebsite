## Policy-compliant link opportunities (NEET counselling, India)

Each is a person-to-person pitch, one at a time. Lead with a specific finding, not "please link to us". Names below are categories and examples to research, not confirmed contacts.

1. News and data journalism (highest value). Pitch the PG stipend report (state-wise stipend, government vs private) to health and education desks: national dailies' education desks, Medical Dialogues, ThePrint, health-policy reporters. Stipend disparity is a recurring story. Offer the CSV plus a quotable counsellor. Time it to a peg such as a resident-doctor stipend dispute, an NMC stipend directive, or MCC round dates.
2. Medical student and resident bodies, by contributing rather than dropping links. Resident doctors' associations (FAIMA, FORDA, UDF, state RDAs) publish stipend campaigns and need comparative data. Offer the dataset as a reference. On Reddit and Quora: genuine answers only, with disclosed affiliation; expect nofollow, still worth referral traffic and brand mentions.
3. Coaching institutes and educators (NEET PG/UG prep platforms and YouTube educators). Offer branch cutoff and fee datasets for their counselling-session material; they cite sources for credibility.
4. Academic and institutional resource pages (.edu.in, .ac.in, .nic.in): medical college career cells, university admission-info pages, school career-counselling "useful links" lists. Low yield, so pitch only relevant pages found by searching e.g. `site:ac.in "NEET" "useful links"`, and offer the open cutoff and fee datasets, not the commercial pages.
5. Data and reference ecosystems. schema.org Dataset markup already enables Google Dataset Search. For Wikipedia, do not add links in bulk; where an article cites outdated stipend or cutoff figures, raise it on the talk page and let editors decide.
6. Student blogs, newsletters and Telegram/WhatsApp channel admins covering NEET counselling. Offer the embeddable widgets so they get a live table and you get a source link.
7. Expert-quote requests (journalist query platforms). Answer with data-backed comments from named counsellors.
8. Own profiles (low value, legitimate). Add X, LinkedIn and YouTube in Admin content settings so they flow into `sameAs`; set up Google Business Profile if eligible.
