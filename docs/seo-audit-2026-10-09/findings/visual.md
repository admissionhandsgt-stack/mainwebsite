# Visual audit - www.admissionhands.com (2026-10-09)
Viewports: desktop 1440x900, mobile 390x844 @2x (touch). Screenshots in ../screenshots/ (<page>-<desktop|mobile>-first / -above-fold / -full; plus mobile-locked, pgcutoff-mobile-table, pgcollege-mobile-locked-unscrolled).

## HIGH
1. PG college page, mobile: locked-summary section is 764px wide in a 390px viewport. Root cause: grid `grid gap-8 lg:grid-cols-[1fr_320px]` has an implicit auto track, so the `min-w-[500px]` quota table inside `.ah-gated-depth` (div.overflow-x-auto px-5, 762px) blows the track out to 764px (container 358 -> 780 scrollWidth). html/body are overflow-x:hidden so no scrollbar, content is just clipped: "As published by the counselling authority. 'Widest' is..." and "7 cutoff rows - branch, quota, category and the rank it..." cut mid-word, the CLOSING RANKS and fee columns and the "Show me all 7 cutoff rows" CTA (centered at x~570) are off-screen. The entire persuasion/conversion point of the locked state is unreachable on a phone. Fix: `grid-cols-1` / `grid-cols-[minmax(0,1fr)]` on that grid (or min-w-0 on the track) and let the table scroll inside its own box. Check the sibling sections on the same grid (stipend/hostel heading is also pushed).
2. Locked summary on mobile hides its own payload: on the UG college page the quota table is `min-w-[500px]` inside a 358px box, so only QUOTA and SEATS show; the CLOSING RANKS column (the only thing that proves the data exists) is scrolled off with no cue. Same on /neet-pg-cutoff: table min-w-[520px], "LAST ADMITTED" (the headline figure, 1,87,915) is off-screen, only Category / Colleges / Round 1 close visible. Fix: stack rows as cards on <640px, or fade-edge/scroll hint, or drop to 3 columns.
3. UG college locked state shows "SEATS 0" for Deemed and Management quotas while listing rank ranges (9,18,859 - 11,82,026). Reads as broken data and undercuts the "we have the data" pitch. Also the "BEST ROUND 1 CLOSE - 2026 = 5,93,506 / The tightest seat here" card, MBBS INTAKE "-" and TYPE "other" (lowercase, unknown) make a thin page look unfinished. Hide zero/unknown fields instead of printing them.

## MEDIUM
4. Homepage mobile above the fold: value proposition is clear ("MBBS & PG admission in India, made simple", "Real cutoffs, not estimates", 3 proof chips) and primary CTA "Get expert guidance" (y=478, 48px) is visible, but the seat predictor - the product - is not offered above the fold on mobile (only "Browse top colleges"). Desktop hero also lacks it (nav item only). A rank-entry box or "Predict my seat" CTA is the obvious primary action for this audience. Also ~150px of dead space between the buttons and the three trust chips, and the chips are stacked narrow pills, pushing the fold; the sticky header (72px) + updates ticker (40px) eat 112px of 844.
5. /mbbs-india mobile: image credit ("Government Medical College, Thiruvananthapuram - (c) Adnan Haleem - CC BY-SA 3.0") overlaps/touches the bottom of the "Call Expert" button (button bottom ~507px, caption ~498px). CTA buttons are 40px high (<44px; Secure Your MBBS Seat 40, Call Expert 40). /md-ms-india mobile: "Talk to a PG Expert" 36px high, WhatsApp Us 40px. Both under the 44-48px target.
6. Predictor mobile above the fold: H1 + 6-line intro paragraph push the rank input to y~650 and "Find my colleges" to y~718 of 844 (visible, but only just; the Branch filter is at the fold). Cut the paragraph or move the input above it. Button is greyed (disabled look) until a rank is typed - reasonable but low-contrast pale cyan with white text.
7. Floating Call/WhatsApp circles (bottom-left/right, 44px+) sit over content on every page, covering text in the locked card ("Your phone number, once. No payment...") and chip rows. Hidden "Call Expert"/"WhatsApp us" buttons (0x0/2x2) exist in the DOM at y=810 on predictor and college pages (visually-hidden sticky bar?) - verify they are not announced to screen readers/tapped.
8. /md-ms-india desktop: CLS 0.100 at ~700ms (hero/ticker text shift) - at the "needs improvement" threshold; others 0 - 0.007. First paint vs settled looked identical at 600ms in screenshots, so it is the font swap/ticker, not a visible jump.

## Passed
- No page-level horizontal overflow reported at 390 or 1440 (but see HIGH 1: it is hidden by overflow-x:hidden, not absent).
- Header/hamburger/theme controls are 44px. No console page errors. CLS <=0.007 on all but /md-ms-india desktop.
- Locked state copy is reasonable: lock icon, "Sign in with your phone number to see the rows", per-quota counts, honest caveat, clear CTA "Show me all N cutoff rows" and "Your phone number, once. No payment, and no password the first time." On UG (358px) the card fits and reads well; the persuasion weakness is the missing rank column and 0-seat rows, not the wording.
