# Google Business Profile — setup kit (2026-10-09)

Google does not allow a new profile to be created by API: its Business Profile API is only granted to
a profile that is already verified and has been active for 60+ days. So the owner creates it by hand
(10–15 minutes) at **https://business.google.com/create**, signed in with the business Google account
(Admissionhands.gt@gmail.com is a good choice — the same account that owns the other business tools).
Everything to type is below. **Copy it exactly** — the name, address and phone must match the website
character for character (Google compares them).

## 1. Basics

| Field | Enter |
|---|---|
| Business name | Exactly what is written on the office signboard — e.g. `AdmissionHands` or `Admission Hands`. No extra words like "NEET counselling" (Google suspends keyword-stuffed names). |
| Primary category | `Educational consultant` |
| Additional category | `Career guidance service` (if the picker shows it) |
| Do you want to add a location customers can visit? | **Yes** |
| Address | `915, Bhutani City Center, Sector 32` · City `Noida` · State `Uttar Pradesh` · PIN `201301` |

| Phone | `+91 93103 01949` |
| Website | `https://www.admissionhands.com/contact` |

## 2. Verification (the part only the owner can do)

Google will most likely ask for a **video**. In one continuous take, show:
1. The street / building outside (Bhutani City Center signage),
2. Walking to **suite 915** and the **AdmissionHands signboard** at the door,
3. Inside the office, and something that proves you run it (unlocking the door, a business document,
   GST/registration certificate, or the office's electricity/rent bill).
Put the signboard up **before** recording — a shared office building with no sign is the most common
reason verification fails. Approval usually takes 1–5 working days.

## 4. Reviews (the biggest local ranking factor after the address)

From the profile, copy the "Ask for reviews" link and send it on WhatsApp to students you have
already helped — 2–3 a week, not a burst. Reply to every review. **Never** buy reviews, offer anything
for one, or post reviews yourselves; Google removes them and can suspend the profile.

## 5. Tell us when it is live

Send the profile's share link (Share → copy link). It goes into the website's `sameAs` so Google
connects the profile, the site, Facebook, Instagram and YouTube as one business.
